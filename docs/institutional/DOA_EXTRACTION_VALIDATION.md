The existing geometry-aware parser assigns role words to actual table columns and preserves qualifiers/unresolved cells. The new DoARecord separates business activity, category, provision/page/version, preparer, reviewer, recommender, approver and executor. Other documented roles remain separate. Requester/process/financial thresholds/restrictions/exception paths/escalation requirements stay null unless supported; qualifier text is retained without inventing an interpretation. A blank cell is never assigned an approver. Consultation/recommendation/review are not approval.

Private lookup supports Arabic normalization, activity/body/category/role and source filters. The public Authority Explorer uses three fictional activities with clearly fictional bodies. It cannot answer institutional authority from private data. Source occurrence, hash identity, version declaration, approval, effective date, supersession and permission are separate fields/statuses.

Validation includes role separation, missing-value preservation, source binding, page/text occurrence, corruption denial, version collisions, future dates and superseded sources.
