# Authorization-aware brand registry

Only two standalone original variants have been processed and registered: horizontal bilingual artwork for light and dark backgrounds. Their original geometry, colors, aspect ratios and bytes are preserved. A vertical bilingual mark was observed on the DoA cover; it is not registered as a new standalone export asset or redrawn from a composite page.

The public registry records identifier, original source, hash, languages, orientation, intended use, color/background restrictions, unknown minimum size/safe area, approval status, publication status, original version and storage location. Public assets require USER_DECLARED_PUBLIC and a matching hash. Publication permission is distinct from design approval. The private registry adds protected relative storage locations and validates `brand.read` on each access. Private bytes are supplied to the draft renderer only after case/export and brand authorization succeed.

No unsupported Arabic-only, English-only, vertical, symbol-only, footer or wordmark variant is invented. No logo recoloring, glass sculpture, reflections inside the mark or altered proportions are introduced. Glass effects apply to interface surfaces and icon containers.

The supplied eight-page identity PDF is not evidence that every token from the earlier unprovided 87-page guide has been verified. Current digital tokens are creative extensions pending institutional review. Noto/Carlito browser/document fallbacks are operational fonts, not claims of approved official typography. Production use, minimum-size/safe-area rules and new variants require verified supporting records.
