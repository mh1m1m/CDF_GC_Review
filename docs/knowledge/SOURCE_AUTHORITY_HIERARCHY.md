# Source authority and unresolved hierarchy

Accessibility, a public URL, a familiar logo or a newer filename cannot establish approval or authority. The existing source provider's approval/effective/supersession checks remain authoritative metadata. Answers preserve unverified status.

Conflict records identify competing quotations, IDs/pages/versions and the gap in established hierarchy. A later version does not automatically supersede another without verified supersession. Overlapping departmental tasks do not establish one exclusive owner. Both conditions produce an escalation, not an invented institutional conclusion. The deterministic fixture scenarios explicitly remain fictional and nonbinding.

A future institutional deployment must supply trusted current approvals, source currency, hierarchy and authorizer records through the existing private integration ports. The copilot cannot create them. No recommendation may supersede a validated authority matrix.
